Hiya. Been a while!

If you've been following us for a while now, you might have noticed that nothing's been going on, and there's been a lack of updates or news from us for a couple months now.

I promise there's good reasons for all of this, and I haven't been lazy! I'm still actively working on stuff, but in software development there isn't always something crazy or flashy to show. Most of my work is on slow, boring stuff that I don't believe is worthy enough to share to the general populace.

Regardless, there's a lot to talk about!

## Team Downsizing

You might've noticed that we've lost a couple members of the team.

No one left on any bad terms. People simply either lost interest or are too busy elsewhere to contribute or stay on the team, and thus decided to part ways amicably.

## So what have I been doing?

For my personal projects, I've pretty much moved on from s&box. I might work with it still on certain projects, especially if I'm helping others, but for personal solo projects I have moved on to my own engine, which I've been working on for the past 5 months now.

There's still a bunch of work to be done on it, but I'm really proud of all the progress I've made this far. I'm so excited to see what the future holds for it.

Already it has gotten to the point where you can make games with it, but it is lacking a lot of polish and features. I strongly believe that it's at a point already where I can start dogfooding it, seeing what stinks, and gradually improving.

I might release the engine to the public eventually, but I'm not sure yet. In my mind I want to give people more reason to support me on my ko-fi page, so perhaps I will do supporter releases? Regardless, I'm very happy with the progress on it that I've managed to make.

## So what happens to all of my s&box projects?

The plan is to either cut development on them entirely or move them over to my own game engine.

I have played around with ideas in my mind on how I would remake them using my own engine, but I haven't made up my mind yet. I'm still thinking about it.

All s&box libraries and projects currently up will not be receiving any more updates. I'm thankful for everyone who's played and supported my work on s&box, and I still really like the engine, however for my personal projects I find little reason to use it anymore.

## What's next?

You can expect me to always keep working on stuff! I still very much love what I do and I want to keep doing it for as long as I can.

I will keep working on the game engine, and in the coming months there will be more news regarding my future game projects as they get further along.

If you want to financially support me to keep going, I have a ko-fi page, you can find it [here](https://www.ko-fi.com/kelliedev). I don't like talking about it much because I feel like a beggar, but if you have money to spare and love what I do, I'd REALLY appreciate it. Also if you have feasible ideas for new perks, I'd love to hear it. I want to give everyone who supports me as much as possible.

Regardless, that's pretty much all I wanted to say. Expect more news to come soon. Sorry for the wait!

Thank you all so much, see you soon!! <3
